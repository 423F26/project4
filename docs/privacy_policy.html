CatPack Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Applies to the CatPack app (package com.CatPack.liftingapp) on Android, and to the CatPack app for iPhone.

CatPack is a strength-training tracker for sports teams. Coaches and athletes log lifts, follow progress and compare results on leaderboards. The app is developed and operated by Garrett Miller and Gaige Bache ("the CatPack team", "we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

In short

  • We collect what you type into the app: your account details, your athlete profile, and the lifts and workouts you log.
  • Your teammates and coaches see your team data. Your best lifts from General use appear on a leaderboard other signed-in users can see, unless you turn that off.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and asks for no device permissions. We don't sell your data.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in and security data. Firebase Authentication, the Google service that signs you in, logs technical data such as your IP address, device type and sign-in times to protect accounts and prevent abuse. It may also run an automated check that the request comes from a genuine app and device.
  • Data kept on your phone. The app saves your settings (such as theme and the last team you opened), some workouts, and a copy of recent data for offline use. If Android backup is switched on for your phone, Android may include this on-device data in the backup held in your own Google account.

What we don't collect

CatPack doesn't collect your location, contacts, health-sensor data, microphone or camera input, or advertising ID. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and it requests no runtime permissions.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, confirm that you own the email address you sign up with (we email you a verification link), sign you in, and let you reset your password.
  • To provide the app's features: saving your lifts and workouts, drawing your progress chart, ranking leaderboards (body weight is used for pound-for-pound rankings), running teams, and delivering workouts sent in the app.
  • To sync your data between your devices.
  • To approve coach requests and administer accounts.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell it.

Sending workouts by text or email. When you choose to text or email a workout, CatPack opens your phone's own messaging or email app with the message filled in. You send it yourself, and it travels through that app and your carrier or email provider, not through us.

4. Who we share data with

  • Google (service provider). CatPack runs on Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Cloud Firestore for storage. Your data is stored on Google's servers, which may be located in the United States or other countries, and Google processes it on our behalf. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of users or the security of the service.
  • If the project changes hands. If responsibility for CatPack passes to another team or organization, your data would move with it under this policy, and we would tell you in the app or on this page.

We don't share your data with advertisers, data brokers or any other third parties.

5. Your privacy settings

In the app, tap your profile picture to open Account & settings. Under Privacy & visibility you can:

  • turn Show me on the General leaderboard off, which removes your rows from it;
  • turn Use my initials there on, to show "J. S." instead of your full name;
  • turn Show my profile picture to others off;
  • turn Teammates can see my progress off, so only you and your coaches can open your chart and workout log.

On the same screen you can edit your name, position group, body weight and phone number, change or remove your profile picture, and leave a team. You can edit or delete individual sets from the Log tab.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Account & settings, scroll to Account, and tap Delete account. If you signed in more than a few minutes ago, the app asks you to sign in again first.

Without the app: contact us from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your sign-in, profile and profile picture;
  • everything you logged in General use, and your General leaderboard rows;
  • your own athlete profile on each team, with its sets and workout log, and your team memberships;
  • your own workouts, workouts friends sent you, your follows (and your name in other people's followers lists), and your own followers list.

Some things stay because they belong to a team rather than to you:

  • teams you created, so your teammates keep their data;
  • athletes you added to a team roster as a coach, and their lifts;
  • workouts you shared with or sent to a team.

To have any of these removed as well, or to have a profile that a coach created about you removed, ask that team's coach or contact us.

If an administrator deletes an account, we keep a minimal record (the account's internal ID and who removed it) so the sign-in can't be reused.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by contacting us.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, or ask us to, the data listed above is deleted from our live database straight away. Copies in Google's backup systems are removed on Google's own schedule. Technical sign-in logs kept by Firebase Authentication are held by Google for a limited period.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and Google's servers and encrypted at rest by Google. Access rules on the database limit each person to their own data and the data of teams they belong to. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security; if you think your account has been misused, contact us.

9. Children

CatPack is intended for college-level athletes and their coaches. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change significantly affects how your data is used, we'll also tell you in the app.
