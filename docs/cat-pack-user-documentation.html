Before you start

  • A phone: any Android phone or tablet running Android 7.0 or newer. There's no iPhone version.
  • An internet connection to sign in. Logging and viewing lifts work on what's already saved on the phone.
  • The app itself. The Beta isn't on the Google Play Store. Ask the MSU Lift Tracker team to install it on your phone or to send you the install file. If you open an install file, Android may ask you to allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app. Developers can build it themselves with the Developer Guide.
  • Pounds: all weights in the app are in pounds (lb).

Get started

Create an account

  1. Open LIFTING APP from your home screen. The sign-in screen appears.
  2. Tap New here? Create an account.
  3. Enter your Name, Email and a Password of at least 6 characters.
  4. Tap Create account. The app opens on the Log tab.

Prefer your Google account? Tap Continue with Google on the sign-in screen and pick the account.

You stay signed in until you sign out, so next time the app opens straight to the Log tab.

Forgot your password?

  1. Type your email address on the sign-in screen.
  2. Tap Forgot password? The app shows Reset link sent to followed by your address.
  3. Open the email, set a new password, then sign in with it. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

Find your way around

The top of the screen has the app's logo and name, the theme button and the sign-out button . Below that are three tabs:

  • Log: pick an athlete and a lift, log sets, and see that athlete's progress chart.
  • History: every set logged on this phone, newest first.
  • Leaderboard: the athletes ranked for one lift.

Log your first set

Each set belongs to an athlete, so add your athletes first. You only do this once per athlete.

Add an athlete

  1. On the Log tab, tap Add athlete.
  2. Enter the athlete's Name, Position group (for example OL, WR or DB) and Body weight (lb).
  3. Tap Add. The button stays grey until all three are filled in and the body weight is above 0. The new athlete is now selected in the Athlete picker.

Double-check the body weight. It's used for pound-for-pound rankings, and in the Beta you can't change it later.

Log a set

  1. Choose the athlete in the Athlete picker. Then choose the lift in the exercise picker: Bench, Squat, Deadlift, Power Clean, Pull-ups or Push-ups.
  2. Tap Log set at the bottom right. The dialog title names the lift and athlete, for example Log Bench for Jordan Smith.
  3. Enter the Weight (lb) and the Reps (1 to 20). Once both are valid, the dialog previews the estimate, for example Estimated 1RM: 263 lb for 225 × 5.
  4. Tap Save. A line under the chart confirms it, for example Saved Jordan Smith: Bench 225 × 5.

The chart, the History tab and the Leaderboard all update right away. The set is stamped with the current date and time.

If you tap Log set before adding any athletes, the app opens Add athlete first.

Track progress

The chart on the Log tab shows one athlete and one lift, with one point for each day they trained. The real dates are spaced out, so a gap between sessions looks like a gap.

On the chartWhat it means
Solid line, Est. maxThe best estimated max from that day's sets
Dashed line, Heaviest setThe heaviest weight lifted that day, at any number of reps
Best est. max (big number at the top)The highest estimated max in view, also marked by a dashed line labeled Best
Since a date, with + lbHow far the best day in view is above the first day in view
1 set or 12 setsEvery set this athlete has logged for this lift, including sets outside the chosen range
  • Change the time range with 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y or All under the chart. If nothing falls in that range, the chart says so; tap All to see every set.
  • Read a single day by tapping a point on the solid line. A label shows the date, the estimated max and the set it came from. If that day also had a heavier set, the label shows its weight too.
  • Move along the timeline by dragging sideways. Pinch to zoom in on a stretch of time. Scrolling up and down still scrolls the page.

Fix a mistake

Typed 525 instead of 225? You can change or remove a set from the chart.

  1. On the Log tab, tap the chart point for the day the set was logged.
  2. Tap Edit or delete set from … under the range buttons.
  3. If that day has more than one set, pick the one you want from the list.
  4. Tap Edit to change the weight or reps and then Save. Or tap Delete, which removes the set right away.
  5. A message at the bottom of the screen offers Undo for a few seconds. Tap it if you changed your mind.

Editing only changes the weight and reps. If a set went to the wrong athlete or lift, delete it and log it again under the right one.

History

The History tab lists every set logged on this phone, newest first. Each row shows:

  • the athlete
  • the lift, weight and reps
  • the date and time
  • the estimated max

The list is read-only. To change a set, use the chart on the Log tab (see Fix a mistake).

Leaderboard

  1. Open the Leaderboard tab and choose a lift. This picker is separate from the one on the Log tab.
  2. Choose how to rank:
    • Estimated max: ranks athletes by their best estimated max for this lift.
    • Pound-for-pound: divides that estimated max by the athlete's body weight, so lighter athletes can compete fairly.

Each athlete appears once, using their single best set. Athletes with no sets for that lift don't appear. Each row also shows the athlete's body weight (BW).

Example: Bench. Two athletes trade places depending on the ranking.

Themes and signing out

Change the colors: tap at the top of the screen. Choose Blue & Gold, Graphite or Daylight. The app remembers your choice.

Sign out: tap at the top right, then Sign out. You go back to the sign-in screen.

Signing out doesn't delete any athletes or lifts. They stay on the phone (see Limits of the Beta).

Limits of the Beta

These are known limits of the current version, not problems with your phone:

  • Data stays on one phone. Athletes and lifts are saved only on the phone where they were entered. They don't sync to other phones, and anyone who signs in on that phone sees the same roster. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Athletes can't be edited or removed. A name, position or body weight stays as it was first entered.
  • Body weights are visible. Anyone using the phone can see every athlete's body weight on the Leaderboard.
  • Body-weight exercises need added weight. Pull-ups and Push-ups need a weight above 0, and every set is limited to 20 reps.
  • Pounds only. There's no kilogram setting.
  • No coach features yet. Every account is treated the same.
  • Display issues:
    • The solid Est. max line can be hard to see. Tap points to read their values.
    • The 1 on the first-place Leaderboard badge may not show, but the top row is always first.
    • The Since figure never shows a drop in strength.

Terms

Set
One effort at one weight for a number of reps, written weight × reps (for example 225 × 5).
Estimated max (e1RM)
An estimate of the most an athlete could lift once, from the Epley formula: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). A single rep counts as the weight itself.
Pound-for-pound
The estimated max divided by body weight. For example, 1.48× BW means 1.48 times the athlete's body weight.
Heaviest set
The most weight lifted on a day, whatever the number of reps.
Position group
The athlete's position on the team (for example OL or DB). It's shown next to their name.
